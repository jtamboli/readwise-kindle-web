Kindle Test Pages

Test pages for evaluating navigation patterns and fonts on Kindle Paperwhite's experimental browser.

Navigation Patterns

  • Touch Zones
    Fixed left/right zones. Tap left (20%) to scroll up, tap right (80%) to scroll down.
  • Inline Chunk Links
    Subtle "continue" links inserted every half-screen. Minimal visual intrusion.
  • Full-Width Block Links
    Large tap-target blocks between content sections. Maximizes tap accuracy.
  • Two-Column Navigation
    Fixed sidebar with section links. Experimental - may render poorly on e-ink.
